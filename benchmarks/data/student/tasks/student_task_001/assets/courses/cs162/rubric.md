# CS162 Byzantine Fault Tolerance Essay Rubric

1. Quorum Proof (40%): Must explicitly prove why 3f + 1 nodes are needed to tolerate f faulty/malicious nodes.
2. Failure Models (30%): Distinguishes crash-stop failures from arbitrary/Byzantine failures.
3. Consensus Protocol Comparison (30%): Compares PBFT or Raft trade-offs.
